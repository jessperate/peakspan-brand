The definitive reference for PeakSpan's visual identity - colors, typography, components, and design tokens extracted from the live brand system.

Logo

The PeakSpan wordmark features a custom geometric "A" with a mountain-peak negative space. The three dots beneath the "A" are a distinctive brand element that should never be removed.

Color Variants

Against White Background
Logo: #074469 (--ps-dark-800)
Against Collage / Light Background
Logo: #074469 (--ps-dark-800)
Against Dark Blue Background
Logo: #ffffff (white)

Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "P" character. This ensures the mark is never crowded by surrounding elements.

P-height
P-height
P-height
P-height

Minimum Size

Digital min: 100px wide
Print min: 1 inch wide

Logo Misuse

Don't

  • Rotate, skew, or warp the wordmark
  • Remove or reposition the three dots under the "A"
  • Change the logo color to anything outside the approved variants
  • Add drop shadows, gradients, or effects
  • Place the dark logo on dark backgrounds (use white version)
  • Stretch or compress the aspect ratio
  • Outline or stroke the wordmark
  • Place on busy imagery without sufficient contrast

Do

  • Use dark blue (#074469) on white or light backgrounds
  • Use white (#ffffff) on dark blue or dark photography
  • Maintain clear space equal to the P-height on all sides
  • Scale proportionally using the SVG source
  • Ensure sufficient contrast against collage or image backgrounds
  • Center-align for hero usage, left-align for nav

Collage Imagery

PeakSpan uses a signature mixed-media collage style that combines portrait photography, mountain landscapes, and torn paper/fabric textures. Two primary formats: people features and subpage headers.

People Collage (Portfolio Founders)

1
Background Canvas
Neutral warm tone (#f6f6ee or similar). Provides breathing room around the composition.
2
Texture Swatches
5-7 torn paper, fabric, or stone fragments. Muted earth tones and cool grays. Scattered organically, slightly rotated. Some peek behind the subject, others in front.
3
Mountain Landscape
A dramatic peak or ridge, partially visible behind the subject. Reinforces the "PeakSpan" brand metaphor of scaling summits.
4
Person (B&W Cutout)
Black-and-white or desaturated portrait, cleanly cut out from background. Shoulders-up or waist-up framing. Centered in composition, overlapping the landscape.
5
Nameplate Label
White rectangle, centered below the portrait. Name in Roboto Mono uppercase (0.14em tracking). Company logo directly beneath. No border on the label itself.

When to Use

Do

  • Portfolio company founder/CEO spotlights
  • Team member features on the People page
  • Case study hero images
  • Conference speaker cards
  • Annual report leadership profiles
  • Use desaturated or B&W portraits for the subject
  • Keep textures muted - earthy tones, cool grays, soft fabrics
  • Vary the mountain landscape per person for uniqueness

Don't

  • Use for generic stock photography or decorative filler
  • Use color portraits - always desaturate or convert to B&W
  • Over-saturate the texture swatches - keep them natural
  • Use more than one person per collage composition
  • Add drop shadows to the cutout or texture pieces
  • Use the collage style on small thumbnails or icons
  • Mix PeakSpan brand colors into the collage textures

Subpage Header Collage

Used as hero banners on interior pages (Portfolio, Team, Insights). Combines the mountain collage with figures at smaller scale, set against a light brand-blue gradient background.

1
Gradient Background
Light brand-blue gradient (--ps-050 to --ps-200). Provides an airy, aspirational feel. Full-width banner, typically 16:5 aspect ratio.
2
Right-Aligned Collage
Mountain landscape with small hiking figures, layered with muted color blocks (lavender, blush, slate). Composition bleeds off the right edge. Figures are small-scale, reinforcing the "scaling the peak" metaphor.
3
Left-Aligned Text
Montagu Slab headline at display size, DM Sans body copy beneath. Left-aligned, occupying roughly 50-55% of the banner width. Text should not overlap the collage composition.

1
Organic Shape
Each collage is masked to an irregular, torn-paper outline. No rectangular bounding boxes. The silhouette should feel hand-cut, not geometric.
2
Layered Depth
Color blocks (lavender, blush, slate) sit behind the mountain photo. Stone/rock textures can anchor the base. Torn paper edges poke out from behind the landscape layer. Grid-paper and fabric swatches add tactile variety.
3
Sizing
Simple peaks for small decorative accents (200-300px). Larger compositions with figures for hero-adjacent placements (400-600px). Always float on the background with generous whitespace.
4
Optional Figures
Small hikers (1-2 max) can be added to larger compositions. They should be tiny relative to the mountain, reinforcing scale and ambition. Place them on ridgelines or ascending slopes.
